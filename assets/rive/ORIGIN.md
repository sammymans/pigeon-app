# Artwork provenance

The pigeon, leather aviator helmet, goggles, postal envelope, blue contrail, padded catch net, feathers, and delivery seal were drawn as original 2D vector geometry in this task for Pigeon. No stock Rive file, marketplace animation, font, image, or third-party character artwork was imported.

The palette and product behavior follow Pigeon's supplied PRD section 6 and Aviator Pack requirement. Motion applies the installed 12-principles-of-animation guidance supplied at Sammy's request: anticipation, approximate volume preservation in squash/stretch, staged emphasis, independent-axis curved takeoff, easing, and overlapping secondary letter/tail motion. No missing companion script was assumed to exist or executed.

Actual RML authoring, validation, inspection, and render use Rive's official Linux CLI 1.9.2 from https://releases.rive.app/cli/install.sh, installed after inspecting the script. The installer checks the release archive's SHA-256. The command-line source project contains no scripts. Rive account/export terms still apply; no licensing restriction has been removed or bypassed. Export is pending the owner's free account sign-in, and a free-tier watermark may be imposed.

The SVG fallbacks reproduce the same owned vector paths and static timeline poses; PNG fallback counterparts are rasterized from those SVGs. The preview sheet and video use the genuine Rive renderer, not the SVG fallback renderer. The cream preview backdrop is a presentation aid, absent from the production artboards.
